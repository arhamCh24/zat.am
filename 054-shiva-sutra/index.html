How to play

  1. Select New Game to shuffle the tiles.
  2. Select a tile next to the empty square to slide it.
  3. Arrange the tiles left to right, top to bottom, then submit your solution.

About the sutras

The Shiva Sutras, also called Akṣara samāmnāya, group the sounds of Sanskrit into fourteen short sequences used in Pāṇini’s grammar.
