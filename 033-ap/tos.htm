What is अन्विष्यन्तां पदानि?

It is an internal-use collection of Sanskrit Word Search puzzles published in the monthly magazine सम्भाषणसन्देशः (Sambhashana Sandesha) from 1996 to 1998 by Samskrita Bharati, India. The collection helps volunteers learn Sanskrit and can be used as a teaching aid.
