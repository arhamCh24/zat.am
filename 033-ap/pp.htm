Privacy

The Zat.am navigation bar uses Firebase Authentication to identify signed-in players. For Amuse Labs progress saving, this page hashes the Firebase Auth UID with SHA-256 and sends the pseudonymous value to Amuse Labs as the player's UID. The raw Firebase UID is not sent to Amuse Labs by this page. When a puzzle is completed, Amuse Labs sends the puzzle ID, completion status, time, and score to this page. Zat.am converts the Amuse score to a base score of up to 80 points, then adds a time bonus. For signed-in players, Zat.am submits this score and the attempt metrics to its shared leaderboard, associated with the Firebase account. Results from signed-out players are not saved to the Zat.am leaderboard. Amuse Labs may process and store puzzle progress according to its own service and privacy terms.
