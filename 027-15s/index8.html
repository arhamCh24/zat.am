८ समस्या

Arrange the Sanskrit numbers in order.

Moves: ०

How to play

  1. Press Scramble to mix the tiles.
  2. Click a numbered tile next to the empty space to slide it.
  3. Arrange the numbers from left to right, starting at the top.
  4. Leave the empty space in the bottom-right corner.

Hover over a tile to see its number in English digits.

Solve displays the completed arrangement.
